What's real vs. what's illustrative

Actually runs in this tab
  • Screen / camera / audio recording, download, live waveform, and (where supported) live transcript
  • Video thumbnails and the sort/filter on your library
  • AI summary — calls Claude directly
  • Recording requests full HD and tells Chrome to prioritize sharpness over motion smoothness; the app reports your actual captured resolution afterward so you can verify it
  • "Download HD (MP4/MP3)" loads a real conversion engine (ffmpeg.wasm, ~25 MB, one-time) and re-encodes fully in your browser — nothing is uploaded. It's capped at 1280px wide to keep browser memory in check, and can still fail on longer recordings
  • "Original .webm" keeps your full recorded resolution (e.g. 1080p), downloads instantly, and has no memory limit — the more reliable choice for full quality
Not wired up here
  • User accounts and cross-device syncing
  • Team / channel workspaces
  • Auto-joining Zoom / Teams / Meet calls
  • Calendar sync

These need real accounts, a database, and OAuth — which this static page doesn't have. Recordings also live only in this browser tab and are lost on refresh.

Worth knowing

The honest version.

A few things to weigh before you switch your team over.

Do I need an account or any extension installed?+

No. Everything runs in this browser tab using your browser's built-in screen-capture API. Nothing to sign up for, download, or install.

Is it safe to record sensitive meetings?+

Recording and playback happen locally in your browser, and nothing uploads unless you choose to use the paid AI summary. Still, check your own company's policy before recording anything confidential.

Will my team actually switch to async?+

Teams used to live meetings need a few weeks to build the habit. Start with one recurring meeting and expand once it sticks.
